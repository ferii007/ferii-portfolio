# Contact illustration

Built-in imagegen, transparent watercolor envelope inspired by the contact vignette in the supplied ZIP. Final asset: public/images/contact/envelope.webp (960px wide, WebP quality 85). Annotation remains HTML. Social URLs come from site.contact; LinkedIn is the public profile supplied by the user. Form remains a non-sending demo.

Prompt:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent Contact Me portfolio decorative illustration. Generate ONLY a hand-painted watercolor and colored-pencil illustration of an open envelope, inspired by the contact section of the supplied developer portfolio mockup. One large cream envelope with fine dark navy sketch outlines, its upper triangular flap open behind a cream letter with five short blue handwritten-style horizontal lines, no readable text. Warm tactile watercolor paper grain, soft dimensional folds, slight handmade imperfections, soft contact shadow, pale peach translucent paper patches behind the envelope. Envelope tilted slightly counterclockwise, friendly editorial storybook style, navy/denim blue accents and warm ivory matching the About Me books illustration. Landscape 3:2, envelope occupies left 70% of canvas; right 30% clear transparent room for an HTML handwritten note. All objects wholly visible. No surrounding typography, no decorative rays or arrows, no logo, no UI card, no blue background panel, no watermark. True transparent background.
